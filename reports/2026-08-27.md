## Preliminary SWOT (listing-only, not diligence)

### #1 — MEP Engineering Firm (Irvine area, CA) — $725K
- **S:** Niche specialization (K-12 school MEP engineering) in a less cyclical, public-sector-funded niche; two offices (Orange + LA County); strong disclosed margin (25.8%).
- **W:** "Net income" disclosed, not SDE — true owner cash flow may differ once add-backs are applied; engineering firms carry key-person/relationship risk.
- **O:** School infrastructure spending is a relatively stable public-funding category; room to expand into adjacent public-sector work.
- **T:** Government bid-cycle risk (lumpy revenue); freshness unconfirmed (see flag above).

### #2 — Absentee-Run Commercial Cleaning Co. (NH) — $745K
- **S:** SBA pre-approval already in place per the listing (speeds financing); absentee-run, meaning management is already in place; very high margin (71% of revenue) suggests contract-based recurring work.
- **W:** Small revenue base ($352K) — a high margin on a small base is fragile if one or two contracts churn; commercial cleaning is a low-differentiation, competitive category.
- **O:** Recurring B2B contracts give revenue visibility; fragmented industry offers roll-up potential.
- **T:** Contract renewal and customer concentration risk (unconfirmed from listing); low barriers to entry for competitors.

### #3 — Precision Aviation Tooling & Fabrication (Bergen County, NJ) — $1.1M
- **S:** Serves aerospace/defense/automotive — higher barriers to entry, sticky specialized customers; strong margin (35%).
- **W:** Revenue base is modest (~$959K) for the sector; fabrication shops often carry key-employee/owner-skill dependency.
- **O:** Aerospace/defense demand tailwinds; specialized tooling can support long-term contracts.
- **T:** Likely customer concentration in a few large aerospace/defense accounts (unconfirmed); quality certifications (e.g., AS9100) may raise the bar for a new owner without relevant background.

### #4 — Semi-Absentee Custom Cabinetry (Van Nuys, CA) — $1.2M
- **S:** Established high-end niche (custom cabinetry/finish carpentry) with real scale ($2.09M revenue); semi-absentee structure implies existing staff/management.
- **W:** Thinnest margin of the four (~17%), leaving less cushion if revenue softens; construction-trades revenue is project/backlog dependent.
- **O:** SoCal high-end residential/commercial construction market; semi-absentee model could scale further with an added project manager.
- **T:** Rate/construction-cycle sensitivity; skilled-labor dependency; possible customer concentration (unconfirmed).
